Torium is being built as a sovereign EVM-compatible layer-1 blockchain. It is not an Ethereum L2,
rollup or sidecar for the Torium product backend. The local chain runs its own Cosmos SDK state
machine and CometBFT consensus while exposing Ethereum JSON-RPC and EVM execution.

Only a valueless local development network is active. Devnet, public testnet and mainnet identifiers
are reserved but inactive; there is no public RPC, explorer, bridge or production validator set.

## Execution and consensus

Native balances and value transfers use a single canonical Cosmos bank-backed ledger; contract code
and storage remain EVM state. CometBFT commits a block after strictly more than two-thirds of current
voting power precommits it. Under the stated fault assumptions, this is deterministic commit finality
rather than Ethereum beacon-chain finality or a probabilistic confirmation model.

For SDKs and explorers, one committed block is the local default and the JSON-RPC `finalized` tag
means the latest CometBFT-committed state. RPC acceptance is not a promise of inclusion, and there is
no bounded inclusion-time guarantee.

## Current trust model

Localnet has four validators with voting power `25/25/25/25`, all controlled by one developer
authority. Quorum is 67 of 100. The chain continues with one validator unavailable (75 available)
and safely halts with two unavailable (50 available).

Four processes do not make this environment decentralized. The same developer can stop validators,
replace binaries, reset genesis and control the exposed RPC. Localnet proves application and
consensus behavior only; it does not prove independent operation, economic security, censorship
resistance or production resilience.

## Accounts and the native asset

An EVM `0x...` address and a `torium1...` account address encode the same 20 account bytes. They are
two client representations of one account, not two balances.

- base denomination: `atorium`;
- display denomination: `tTOR` on valueless networks and `TOR` on a future mainnet;
- decimals: 18;
- `1 tTOR = 1 TOR = 10^18 atorium` as a unit conversion; local tTOR has no market value.

The local protocol is genesis-capped and non-inflationary. Transaction fees and existing balances
may be redistributed through staking and distribution; no post-genesis native mint is enabled.

## Transactions, gas and blocks

Torium localnet accepts legacy, access-list and EIP-1559 transactions. Blob transactions are
unsupported because Torium has no Ethereum blob/data-availability layer. The local block gas limit
is 30,000,000 with a 15,000,000 target; a single transaction is capped at 25,000,000 gas. These are
local-development parameters, not throughput or public fee-market claims.

The active modules include EVM, bank, staking, distribution, slashing, evidence, governance and
upgrade. IBC and IBC transfer are explicitly excluded from the current protocol; future bridges are
outside v0.
